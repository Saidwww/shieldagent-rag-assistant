# Phishing basics

Phishing is a social engineering attack where an attacker pretends to be a trusted sender to trick people into revealing credentials, clicking malicious links or opening infected attachments.

Common warning signs: urgent or threatening language, a sender address that does not match the organisation, unexpected attachments, and links whose real destination differs from the displayed text.

Defences: multi-factor authentication, email filtering, security awareness training, and a clear process for reporting suspicious messages.
